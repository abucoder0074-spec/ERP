# ADR 0006 · Procurement approvals and document history

## Context

Approvers need a stable amount and assignment when rules, prices or delegates change. Receipts and invoices must retain the commercial terms in force when stock arrived.

## Decision

Value requests using explicit estimated UZS unit costs. Use the VAT-inclusive UZS total for order approvals. At submission, use matching category rules with amount ranges, falling back to general rules per category. Snapshot the ordered group assignments. Matching rules at the same level and group share a step. Require a separate decision maker at each step and block the requester, including through delegation. A request cannot submit without a matching rule. A rejection needs a comment.

Keep submitted lines and commercial terms immutable. Each amendment is a new pending version and receives its own approval. The current approved version remains usable during review. Activation rechecks receipts posted during review. An amendment must preserve the item, price, tax and FX terms of net received quantities and may adjust the outstanding quantities or dates. Cancelling an order closes its unreceived balance and keeps historical receipts and invoice eligibility.

RFQs record manual dispatch to at least two suppliers. Compare valid complete quotes by their UZS equivalent. Require a selection reason above the lowest eligible total. Store quote prices as dated supplier price history.

## Consequences

Document services lock the order before reading cumulative receipt and invoice quantities. Historical versions and decisions remain readable. Phase 2 exposes invoice readiness for Phase 3 and posts no payments.
